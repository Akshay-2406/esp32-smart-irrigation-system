# Multi-Crop Simulation & Experimental Results

This section documents the simulation, multi-crop threshold testing, and performance validation for the Adaptive Multi-Sensor Predictive Smart Irrigation System.

---

## 1. Crop-Specific Moisture Thresholds & Control Matrix

The system dynamically adapts its decision logic based on crop type, local moisture thresholds, and environmental risk factors (temperature/humidity trends).

| Crop Name | Scientific Name | Moisture Threshold | Key System Behavior & Risk Logic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Paddy** | *Oryza sativa* | 45%[cite: 1] | High water demand. Triggers frequent irrigation; stops automatically at 55% to prevent deep percolation[cite: 1]. |
| **Sugarcane**[cite: 1] | *Saccharum officinarum*[cite: 1] | 40%[cite: 1] | Incorporates Evapotranspiration (ETo) data over a 360-day cycle to prevent crop wilting[cite: 1]. |
| **Groundnut**[cite: 1] | *Arachis hypogaea*[cite: 1] | 35%[cite: 1] | High humidity sensitivity. System flags pest/fungal risk windows to optimize pesticide spraying[cite: 1]. |
| **Ragi**[cite: 1] | *Finger Millet*[cite: 1] | 30%[cite: 1] | Drought-resistant. Idle status maintained most of the time to prevent unnecessary water usage[cite: 1]. |
| **Mango**[cite: 1] | *Mangifera indica*[cite: 1] | Heat Index (>30°C)[cite: 1] | Monitors canopy heat during flowering; triggers micro-irrigation to prevent flower drop[cite: 1]. |
